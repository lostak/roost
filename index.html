    Bonuses & overrides

    Trips, production bonuses, agency overrides and earnings adjustments show up in the statement's commission section but aren't recurring policy residual. They're separated out here (and excluded from the residual figures above) so one-off payouts don't distort the residual trend. Note: some trip / 1099 lines are non-cash — booked as income and offset by a matching chargeback the same week (net $0) — so this total can exceed actual bonus cash received.

    Downline & override income

    Your downline is auto-detected from your commissions: any writing agent who ever appears at level (100) on one of your actual client policies is flagged as your downline (house/accounting lines like interest, bonuses and overrides don't count). Income here counts only pure-override policies — ones you did not personally co-write (the listed writing-agent levels already add up to 100). When you're on the split, the statement lumps your own cut in with the override, so that production can't be cleanly attributed and is left out. "Since" is when each agent first appeared.

    Downline hierarchy

    You at the top, your downline beneath (all at the same level by default). Drag an agent onto another to nest them if they're actually on that agent's downline; drop on You to move them back to the top. Each shows their downline count and override income.

    Adjust downline

    The (100) rule isn't perfect. Remove anyone it flagged by mistake, or add an agent it missed. Changes save to config.json and apply right away.
